## Section 1: FAQ in English (For RAG Retrieval)

### Q1: How many times should my baby move in a day?
**A:** From 28 weeks onwards, your baby should move at least 10 times in a 2-hour period. Most babies have their own pattern. The most important thing is to notice your baby's usual pattern and report if it changes. A common method is the Cardiff Count-to-Ten: start counting at the same time each morning, count until you reach 10 movements. If you don't reach 10 in 2 hours, contact your doctor.

### Q2: My wearable shows heart rate of 95 bpm. Is that normal?
**A:** During pregnancy (especially in the third trimester), a resting heart rate of 80–95 bpm is completely normal. Your heart is pumping 40–50% more blood than usual to support your baby. However, if your heart rate is above 100 bpm while resting, combined with fever or feeling very unwell, please contact your doctor.

### Q3: My oxygen reading is 96%. Should I worry?
**A:** An SpO2 of 96% is acceptable during pregnancy. Values above 96% are normal. If your reading drops below 94%, especially while resting (not during exercise), you should contact your doctor. Make sure you are sitting upright or lying on your left side when measuring — lying flat on your back can reduce SpO2 readings due to the baby pressing on blood vessels.

### Q4: I have a slight fever of 37.8°C. What should I do?
**A:** A temperature between 37.6°C and 37.9°C is a low-grade fever. During pregnancy, even a mild fever needs attention. Stay hydrated, rest, and take paracetamol (Crocin/Panadol — safe in pregnancy) if uncomfortable. Monitor your temperature every hour. If it rises above 38°C or doesn't come down in 4 hours, contact your doctor or go to the hospital. Do not take ibuprofen (Brufen) during pregnancy.

### Q5: What does the "Chorioamnionitis Risk" alert mean?
**A:** This alert means that three things happened together: your temperature is elevated (fever), your heart rate is high (tachycardia), and the baby's heart rate is also high (fetal tachycardia). When all three happen together, it can sometimes indicate an infection inside the womb. This is a serious condition. Please go to the hospital or call your doctor immediately. Do not wait to see if it improves on its own.

### Q6: What does "Fetal Distress Possible" mean?
**A:** This alert means the baby's heart rate is unusual AND the baby is not moving much. This combination can mean the baby is not comfortable. Stop any activity, lie on your left side, drink some water, and count your baby's movements for 30 minutes. If you count fewer than 4 movements in 1 hour, go to the hospital right away.

### Q7: The app says "Dehydration/Exhaustion Warning." What do I do?
**A:** Your heart rate is high but your oxygen and temperature are normal — this pattern often happens when you are dehydrated or very tired. Drink water or ORS (Oral Rehydration Solution) immediately. Rest in a cool place lying on your left side. Your heart rate should come down within 30 minutes. If it doesn't, call your doctor.

### Q8: Is it safe to exercise during pregnancy?
**A:** Moderate exercise is generally safe and beneficial during pregnancy. Walking, yoga, and swimming are excellent choices. Avoid exercises that risk falls, heavy lifting, or lying flat on your back after 16 weeks. Stop immediately if you feel chest pain, severe shortness of breath, dizziness, or reduced fetal movement. Always consult your doctor before starting any new exercise during pregnancy.

### Q9: What foods should I eat during pregnancy for iron and nutrition?
**A:** Iron-rich foods are especially important for Indian pregnant women:
- **Iron:** Spinach (palak), drumstick leaves (moringa), jaggery (gud), dates, lentils (dal), horse gram (huruli)
- **Calcium:** Ragi (finger millet), milk, curd, sesame seeds (til)
- **Protein:** Dal, eggs, fish, paneer, sprouted pulses
- **Folate:** Green leafy vegetables, methi (fenugreek), beetroot
- **Vitamin C (helps iron absorption):** Amla (gooseberry), lemon, tomatoes — eat with iron-rich meals
- Take iron and folic acid supplements as prescribed by your doctor

### Q10: When should I go to the hospital immediately?
**A:** Go to the hospital without delay if you experience:
- Heavy vaginal bleeding
- Severe abdominal pain or constant cramping
- Waters breaking (fluid gushing from vagina)
- Severe headache that does not go away
- Blurred vision or seeing flashes/stars
- Sudden swelling of face, hands, or feet
- High fever (38°C or above)
- Baby not moving (fewer than 10 movements in 2 hours)
- The wearable shows a RED/CRITICAL alert

### Q11: What is a normal baby heart rate?
**A:** A healthy baby's heart rate during pregnancy is between 110 and 160 beats per minute. It is normal for the rate to go up when the baby is active and down when the baby is resting. The wearable's microphone detects the baby's heartbeat through the skin. If the baby's heart rate stays below 100 or above 180 for more than 5 minutes, seek medical attention.

### Q12: I am in my 30th week. What antenatal check-ups are due?
**A:** Standard antenatal care schedule (India, WHO guidelines):
- **Week 24–28:** Blood pressure check, urine protein test, blood sugar (GDM screening), Rh blood group check if not done
- **Week 28:** TT (tetanus toxoid) second dose if not given
- **Week 28–32:** Growth scan ultrasound to check baby's size
- **Week 32–36:** Check baby's position, presentation
- **Week 36–40:** Weekly visits, birth plan discussion
Carry your ANC card to every visit.

## Section 2: Kannada FAQ (ಕನ್ನಡ)

### ಪ್ರ. ನನ್ನ ಮಗು ಒಂದು ದಿನದಲ್ಲಿ ಎಷ್ಟು ಬಾರಿ ಚಲಿಸಬೇಕು?
**ಉ.** 28 ವಾರಗಳ ನಂತರ, ನಿಮ್ಮ ಮಗು 2 ಗಂಟೆಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಕನಿಷ್ಠ 10 ಬಾರಿ ಚಲಿಸಬೇಕು. ಪ್ರತಿದಿನ ಒಂದೇ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಎಣಿಸಲು ಪ್ರಾರಂಭಿಸಿ. 2 ಗಂಟೆಯಲ್ಲಿ 10 ಚಲನೆ ಇಲ್ಲದಿದ್ದರೆ ತಕ್ಷಣ ವೈದ್ಯರನ್ನು ಸಂಪರ್ಕಿಸಿ.

### ಪ್ರ. ನನ್ನ ಬೆಲ್ಟ್ ನಲ್ಲಿ ಜ್ವರದ ಎಚ್ಚರಿಕೆ ಬಂದಿದೆ. ಏನು ಮಾಡಲಿ?
**ಉ.** ನಿಮ್ಮ ತಾಪಮಾನ 38°C ಅಥವಾ ಹೆಚ್ಚಾಗಿದ್ದರೆ ಮತ್ತು ಹೃದಯ ಬಡಿತ ಹೆಚ್ಚಾಗಿದ್ದರೆ, ತಕ್ಷಣ ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆಗೆ ಹೋಗಿ. ಕಾಯಬೇಡಿ.

### ಪ್ರ. ಗರ್ಭಾವಸ್ಥೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಡೆಯಬಹುದೇ?
**ಉ.** ಹೌದು, ನಿಧಾನ ನಡಿಗೆ ಗರ್ಭಾವಸ್ಥೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸಂಪೂರ್ಣ ಸುರಕ್ಷಿತ ಮತ್ತು ಉಪಯುಕ್ತ. ಬಿಸಿಲಿನ ಸಮಯ (ಬೆಳಿಗ್ಗೆ 11 ರಿಂದ ಸಂಜೆ 4 ರವರೆಗೆ) ಹೊರಗೆ ಹೋಗುವುದನ್ನು ತಪ್ಪಿಸಿ.

### ಪ್ರ. ನನ್ನ ಆಮ್ಲಜನಕ ಮಟ್ಟ 95% ತೋರಿಸುತ್ತಿದೆ. ಚಿಂತೆ ಬೇಕಾ?
**ಉ.** 96% ಕ್ಕಿಂತ ಹೆಚ್ಚು ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ. 95% ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಕಡಿಮೆ — ಎಡ ಮಗ್ಗುಲಲ್ಲಿ ಮಲಗಿ ಮತ್ತೆ ಅಳೆಯಿರಿ. 94% ಗಿಂತ ಕಡಿಮೆ ಆದರೆ ವೈದ್ಯರನ್ನು ಕರೆಯಿರಿ.

## Section 3: Hindi FAQ (हिंदी)

### प्र. मेरे बच्चे को एक दिन में कितनी बार हिलना चाहिए?
**उ.** 28 हफ्तों के बाद, बच्चे को 2 घंटे में कम से कम 10 बार हिलना चाहिए। हर रोज एक ही समय पर गिनना शुरू करें। अगर 2 घंटे में 10 हलचल नहीं हो तो तुरंत डॉक्टर से मिलें।

### प्र. बुखार की चेतावनी आई है। क्या करूं?
**उ.** अगर तापमान 38°C से ज्यादा है और साथ में दिल की धड़कन भी तेज है, तो तुरंत अस्पताल जाएं। देर न करें।

### प्र. क्या गर्भावस्था में चलना सुरक्षित है?
**उ.** हां, हल्की सैर बिल्कुल सुरक्षित और फायदेमंद है। धूप में (सुबह 11 बजे से शाम 4 बजे तक) न जाएं।

## Section 4: Sensor Belt — Daily Use Guide (For Priya)

### How to Wear the Belt Correctly

1. **Position:** Wrap the belt snugly around the abdomen, slightly above the navel (fundal position for best fetal HR pickup)
2. **Tightness:** Snug but not tight — you should be able to slip two fingers underneath
3. **Microphone placement:** Ensure the microphone module faces inward (toward skin), positioned over the area where you feel the baby move most
4. **Temperature sensor:** Must be in direct skin contact — no clothing between sensor and skin
5. **Wearing time:** Wear for at least 4 hours per day for meaningful data. Best worn during rest periods.

### Daily Routine (Recommended for Priya)

| Time | Activity | Belt Action |
|---|---|---|
| 8:00 AM | Wake up, morning tea | Wear belt for 1-hour morning reading |
| 8:30 AM | Breakfast | Count fetal movements while sitting (belt on) |
| 10:00 AM | Remove belt if going out in heat | Belt off |
| 2:00 PM | Afternoon rest | Belt on — best time for fetal monitoring |
| 6:00 PM | Evening walk | Belt on — activity monitoring |
| 9:00 PM | Night | Belt on for 1-hour pre-sleep reading |

### Alert Response Guide

| Alert Type | Color | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| CHORIOAMNIONITIS_RISK | 🔴 Red | Go to hospital NOW |
| FETAL_DISTRESS_POSSIBLE | 🔴 Red | Lie on left side, call doctor, go to hospital if no improvement |
| PREECLAMPSIA_ANCILLARY_RISK | 🟡 Yellow | Measure BP at pharmacy/clinic today |
| IUGR_RISK_WATCH | 🟡 Yellow | Schedule ultrasound within 48 hours |
| MATERNAL_DEHYDRATION_EXHAUSTION | 🔵 Blue | Drink water, rest, recheck in 30 minutes |
